Pairpath Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026
Developer: Mathew Malyil
Contact: malyilmathew@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Pairpath is a local board game for iPhone. You play against the phone on your device. This policy explains what Pairpath handles. Pairpath does not use an account and does not connect to a developer-operated server.

2. Information Pairpath processes

Pairpath does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information. Game progress stays in the app on your device for the current match.

3. Local storage

The only setting Pairpath stores is whether sound is muted. That choice stays on your device. Pairpath does not send it anywhere.

4. Advertising, analytics, and purchases

The current version of Pairpath has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. If a later version adds any of these, this policy will be updated first.

5. Data sharing

Pairpath does not sell information. It does not send game data or the mute setting to a Pairpath server. Opening the privacy or support page uses your browser.

6. Data retention and deletion

The mute setting remains until you change it or delete the app. Deleting Pairpath removes that setting from the device.

7. Children's privacy

Pairpath is a general board game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Pairpath's handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email malyilmathew@gmail.com or visit the Pairpath Support page.
