BorderCount helps you record travel, plan future trips, and review supported rule-based residency assessments. It provides informational residency tracking and is not tax, legal, financial, immigration, or official government advice.

Travel logging

Add or edit a trip

Open the Trips tab and use Add Trip. Select the country, enter the arrival and departure dates, and review the inclusive day count before saving. Correct overlapping or incomplete records before relying on an assessment.

Fill a travel gap

When an assessment identifies missing history, follow its action to open Trips and add the requested period. BorderCount does not treat an empty period as proof that no travel occurred.

Guided Assessment

Answer only material questions

Open the assessment action from the Dashboard and answer the questions shown for the selected country and period. If information is missing or a pathway is only partly supported, BorderCount explains the next review action instead of inventing a conclusion.

Check the displayed tax year or measurement period, travel completeness, and rule-support explanation. For important decisions, review the official sources shown in the app with a qualified professional.

Photo Travel Reconstruction

Open Trips and choose the photo reconstruction action when you want to review photo date and location metadata. Allow Photos access only if you want this feature. Suggestions remain reviewable and do not automatically create confirmed trips. Unknown or ambiguous locations require review.

Backup and Restore

Open Settings > Back Up or Restore BorderCount. Create a backup only when you want to save or share it. BorderCount validates a backup and asks for confirmation before replacing current data. Review the restore summary carefully before continuing.

Purchases and restoring Pro

Open Settings > BorderCount Pro to view the current StoreKit-localized yearly product. Purchases and restoration use Apple StoreKit. If a purchase is missing, confirm that you are using the Apple account that made it, check your connection, then use Restore Purchases. Apple purchase availability and account issues may require Apple Support.

Reports and exports

Reports and CSV exports are available from the Reports or Settings areas according to the selected country and tax year. Review the destination and contents before sharing. Reports are informational and are not a substitute for professional advice.

Troubleshooting

  • Update to the latest BorderCount version and restart the app.
  • Check that the selected country and tax year are the ones you intend to assess.
  • Review trips for missing dates, overlaps, duplicate days, and unresolved gaps.
  • For photo reconstruction, check Photos permission and remember that limited access scans only the photos you allowed.
  • Keep backups and exported reports in a location you control before deleting or reinstalling the app.

Privacy questions

BorderCount stores its travel and residency records locally and currently has no developer-operated backend. Photo Travel Reconstruction resolves countries on-device. See the BorderCount Privacy Policy for the complete scope, including Apple services and the optional location-import path.
