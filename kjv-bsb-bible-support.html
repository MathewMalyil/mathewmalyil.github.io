KJV & BSB Bible Offline is an iPhone and iPad Bible reader with the 66-book Protestant Old and New Testament canon: 39 Old Testament books and 27 New Testament books. King James Version (KJV) and Berean Standard Bible (BSB) texts are included on your device.

Reading and everyday features

  • Offline reading: no Internet connection is needed for Bible reading or search.
  • Translation switching: choose KJV or BSB using the translation control. Verse numbering differs at some references, so a verse may be absent in one translation.
  • Search: search the selected translation for words or phrases.
  • Bookmarks: save verses and find them using bookmark search, translation filtering and sorting. Each bookmark retains its translation.
  • Copy / Share: use a verse's context menu to copy its text, copy it with its reference, or open Apple's native sharing interface.
  • Chapter navigation: use Previous / Next Chapter to move between chapters, including across book boundaries.
  • Reading size: choose a comfortable font size using the reader's font-size control.

Daily Verse reminders

Open the bell settings to enable Daily Verse notifications, choose a reminder time and select KJV, BSB or Follow Selected Translation. Notification permission is optional. Tap a newly delivered reminder to open its translation and verse in the reader.

The reminder repeats its selected verse each day until it is rescheduled; it does not automatically select a new verse every day.

If a reminder does not appear

  1. Check that Daily Verse is enabled in the app and that its time and translation mode are set as intended.
  2. Check this app's notification permission in iOS Settings. If permission was denied, enable it there before trying again.
  3. Check Focus and notification-summary settings, which can silence or delay alerts.
  4. Set the reminder a few minutes ahead and wait for a new notification. An older notification can contain an earlier verse or translation.

To stop reminders, turn Daily Verse off in the app or disable this app's notifications in iOS Settings. Local reminders do not require an Internet connection. The app does not currently set a notification sound.

General troubleshooting

If content seems unexpected, check the selected translation and reference first. Restart the app if needed. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: bookmarks and preferences are stored locally and may be lost when the app is deleted.
