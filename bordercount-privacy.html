BorderCount Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026
Developer: Mathew Malyil
Contact: malyilmathew@gmail.com

1. Introduction

BorderCount is a residency tracking, travel planning, and informational rule-based assessment application. This policy explains what BorderCount processes and where that information is handled. BorderCount is not tax, legal, financial, immigration, or official government advice.

2. Information BorderCount processes

  • User profile details and country selections.
  • Travel dates, countries, cities, notes, and planned travel.
  • Residency questionnaire answers, including relevant citizenship, residency, and income-threshold categories supplied by you.
  • Residency assessment results and supporting evidence metadata or files that you add.
  • Photo date and location metadata when you choose Photo Travel Reconstruction.

3. Local storage

BorderCount stores profiles, travel records, planned travel, questionnaire answers, and evidence in local app storage. Residency assessments are calculated from the information recorded in the app. BorderCount currently has no developer-operated backend. A travel ledger, residency result, profile fact, citizenship or income category, and evidence file are not transmitted to a BorderCount server.

4. Travel and residency information

Travel and residency information is used on the device to calculate the supported rule-based assessments and planning views you request. The results are informational estimates and may not reflect every fact relevant to a legal determination.

5. Photo Travel Reconstruction

When you choose this feature and allow Photos access, BorderCount checks permitted photo dates and location metadata on your device. Country resolution uses a bundled offline country-boundary dataset. Photo coordinates used for reconstruction are not sent to a BorderCount server, Apple geocoding, or another geocoding service.

Photo results are supporting evidence only. They do not prove absence, fill unobserved days, or automatically create confirmed travel records. Photo suggestions require your review and confirmation.

6. Location services

Automatic location tracking is not active in the current release. A separate optional travel-import path may use Apple's Core Location and geocoding services when you choose that path and grant permission. This is separate from Photo Travel Reconstruction.

7. Evidence and documents

Evidence metadata and files that you add are stored locally. BorderCount does not upload those records to a developer-operated server. Sharing or exporting them occurs only when you initiate the system sharing or export action.

8. Backups and exports

Backup, CSV, PDF, and Accountant Pack files are created only when you ask BorderCount to create them. You choose whether and where to save or share an exported file. Backup files may contain the local information described above. Attached evidence file binaries are not included in the current backup format.

9. In-App Purchases and Apple StoreKit

BorderCount uses Apple's StoreKit for product information, purchases, and restoring purchases. Apple may process purchase and account information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. BorderCount does not control Apple's services.

10. Third-party services

BorderCount currently has no Firebase, advertising, analytics, attribution, Crashlytics, Sentry, or developer-operated telemetry service. Opening official sources, Privacy, Terms, or Support uses normal system or browser services.

11. Analytics and advertising

BorderCount does not include an analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking pixel, or developer-operated telemetry.

12. Data sharing

BorderCount does not sell your information. It does not send your travel ledger, residency results, profile facts, citizenship or income categories, or evidence files to a BorderCount server. Information can leave the device when you use Apple services, the optional location-import path, StoreKit, official web links, or a system export or sharing action that you initiate.

13. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you remove it through the app, delete the app, or otherwise clear its device storage. Deleting BorderCount normally removes data stored within the app's local container from that device. This does not remove files you previously exported or shared, device/cloud backups managed outside BorderCount, or information processed by Apple services.

14. Children's privacy

BorderCount is a general-purpose planning application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request or collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

15. Security limitations

BorderCount uses the platform's protected app storage and system services, but no device or software storage method is completely secure. Keep your device, backups, and exported files protected. Review any shared report or backup before sending it.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when BorderCount's handling changes. The effective date above will identify the current version.

17. Contact and support

For privacy questions or product help, contact malyilmathew@gmail.com or visit the BorderCount Support page.
