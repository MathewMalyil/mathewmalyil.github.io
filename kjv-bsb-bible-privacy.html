KJV & BSB Bible Offline — Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: October 3, 2026
Developer: Mathew Malyil

This policy explains how KJV & BSB Bible Offline handles information. The app requires no account or registration and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

Offline reading and local storage

Bible text is bundled with the app. Bible reading and search do not require an Internet connection. Bookmarks, bookmark dates, selected translation, font preferences and Daily Verse preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or upload these records to the developer.

Optional notifications

Notification permission is optional and is used only for Daily Verse reminders. You can turn reminders off in the app or disable the app's notifications in iOS Settings. Bible reading remains available without notification permission.

Copy and Share

Copy writes the selected verse to the system clipboard only when you choose a Copy action. Share opens Apple's native sharing interface only when you choose Share. You decide whether to share and which destination to use. Any destination or service you select handles the shared information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Scripture content

The app contains King James Version (KJV) and Berean Standard Bible (BSB) Scripture text. The developer does not claim ownership over public-domain Scripture. Translation information is available in the app's Bible Translations area.

Contact and support

For help or privacy questions, visit the Support page or email malyilmathew@gmail.com. If you choose to email support, the developer receives the information you include so your request can be answered. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

This policy describes the app. Opening this website or sending email uses your browser, the website host or your email provider; those services have their own privacy practices.

Policy updates

If the app's information-handling practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.
