Pairpath is a short local board game. You play against the phone. There is no account and no online play.

How to play

  • A 6 leaves home.
  • An exact roll finishes.
  • Landing on the other color sends that coin back.
  • On your turn, tap the die, then tap a glowing coin if you have a choice.

Sound

Use the speaker button to mute or unmute. Loudness follows your iPhone volume. The mute choice stays on the phone.

A new match

Tap New to start again. When a match ends, the win card also offers New.

Troubleshooting

  • Update to the latest Pairpath version and restart the app.
  • If sound is missing, check the mute button and the iPhone volume.
  • Deleting the app clears the mute setting.

Privacy

Pairpath does not collect personal information. See the Pairpath Privacy Policy.
