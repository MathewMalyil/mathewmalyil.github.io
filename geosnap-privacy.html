Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026

GeoSnap Camera respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Access

GeoSnap Camera may access the following on your device:

• Camera access, to capture photos
• Location access, to add GPS, address, and map details to photos
• Photo library access, to save stamped photos to your device

How Information Is Used

The app uses camera, location, and photo library permissions only to provide core app features. Location data is used to display and stamp relevant information on photos you capture.

Data Collection

GeoSnap Camera does not collect, store, or sell your personal data on external servers.

Third-Party Services

The app may use Apple-provided system frameworks such as Maps and Location Services to display map and address information.

Data Sharing

GeoSnap Camera does not share your personal information with third parties.

Your Choices

You can deny camera, location, or photo library access in your device settings. Some app features may not work correctly without these permissions.
